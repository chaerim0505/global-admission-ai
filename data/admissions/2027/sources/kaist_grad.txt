Eligibility Requirements
A. Country of Citizenship
Applicants must be citizens of countries other than Korea to be eligible for KAIST International Graduate Admissions.
Individuals holding Korean citizenship
are not eligible.
Applicants of Korean descent who hold foreign citizenship must meet one of the following conditions:
Submit official documentation confirming the renunciation of Korean citizenship for both the applicant and their parents. If applicable, renunciation must be completed prior to the start of the application period, along with supporting academic records.
Alternatively, applicants may qualify if they have completed their entire primary and secondary education (from elementary through high school, i.e., Grades 1 through 12) outside of Korea. In such cases, certified graduation certificates and transcripts covering all years must be provided. All years must be completed outside Korea without exception; partial completion outside Korea does not meet this requirement. Please note that international schools located within Korea are not considered as meeting this requirement.
B. Academic Qualification
Bachelor’s degree or equivalent standard by the start of your course for
the Master’s or Master’s-Ph.D. degree program application
Master’s degree or equivalent standard by the start of your course for
the Ph.D. degree program application

b.Three-year Bologna compliant Bachelor’s degree
c.The first cycle qualification in the framework of qualifications for the European Higher Education Area (EHEA)
d.Bachelor’s degree with honors in British pattern education
